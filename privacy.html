Privacy

THE PLAYBOOK is built to keep your family’s notes private.

What we store

  • Logs you enter (daily, behaviour, sleep, therapy, wins, etc.) stay in your browser / device storage on that device.
  • We do not run a cloud account for your tracking data in this version.
  • Community notes you write stay on your device unless a future online community is added.

Payments

One-time unlock payments are processed by Stripe. Card details are handled by Stripe, not by THE PLAYBOOK servers.
