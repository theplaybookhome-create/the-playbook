## Full description
THE PLAYBOOK is a calm companion for parents and carers of neurodivergent children.

Keep the day in one place instead of a pile of notes, school apps and reminders.

FREE
- Daily energy and mood log
- Behaviour, sleep, food, therapy and wins
- Multiple children on one device
- Export and import a backup
- Data stays on this device unless you create an account

£2.99 ONCE — no subscription
- Plain-English insights from your own logs
- A one-page professional report for school, GP or therapy
- Discover tools, community and printable visual supports

This is a family organiser. It is not a medical device and it does not diagnose.
